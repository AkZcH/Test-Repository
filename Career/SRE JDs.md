The top 1% of Site Reliability Engineers (SREs) aren't just experts at Kubernetes, AWS, or Terraform. They're engineers who understand how complex systems fail, how to prevent cascading failures, and how to build infrastructure that remains reliable at scale.

If your goal is to become an elite SRE, especially in AI infrastructure, these are the skills that matter.

## What separates the top 1%?

They don't just know tools. They can design, diagnose, and improve production systems from first principles. They automate toil, reason about failure modes, and make reliability measurable.

For your AI infrastructure direction, add GPU observability, inference serving, GPU memory and utilization, model-serving latency, and distributed workload scheduling.

## Your priority order

1. Linux and networking
    
2. Go and Python
    
3. Operating systems and distributed systems
    
4. AWS and Terraform
    
5. Docker and Kubernetes
    
6. Prometheus, Grafana and OpenTelemetry
    
7. SLOs, incident response and chaos engineering
    
8. AI infrastructure and GPU systems
    

One reality check: collecting certifications and learning 30 tools won't make you elite. Build systems, deliberately break them, diagnose failures, and demonstrate measurable reliability improvements.
