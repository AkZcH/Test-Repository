### For your AI Infrastructure Engineer goal

Don't learn these sequentially as if all 182 have equal importance.

Your **core path** should be:

**LLMs → RAG → Evaluation/Harnessing → Agents → Model Engineering → Serving → GPUs → Kubernetes → Distributed Inference → Performance → Observability → Security → AI Infrastructure Architecture**

And importantly, **harnessing/evaluation is not a side topic**. It's one of the major distinctions between someone who merely builds an LLM demo and someone who engineers reliable AI systems.
