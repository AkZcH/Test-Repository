### What is Engineering
- It is a professional discipline that combines knowledge from **Physics, Mathematics, material science along with innovation, analysis and Design** to develop technologies and solution that meet human needs in a safe, efficient and sustainable way
- Engineering needs to solve a human problem or make their lives easier
- Engineering isn't just about knowledge. its about using that knowledge to create practical solutions
- Engineering 
	as a body of knowledge (discipline)
	as a career path (profession)
---
### Engineering as a Discipline
branch of academic study and systematic knowledge that applies scientific and mathematical principles to solve real world problems.

A discipline is an organized body of knowledge to be be studies, developed and researches upon

e.g- Computer Science is a Discipline comprising of DSA, OS,DBMS, CN, DOS, etc. 

#### Characteristics of Engineering as a Discipline
- **Interdisciplinary Nature-** Combining knowledge from different fields
- **Mathematical and Scientific foundation-** relies on scientific principles and math calculations
- **Design, analysis and experimentation-** to validate their solutions
- **Continual Improvement and Life long learning-** update knowledge and tech progresses and evolves.

#### Key components of Engineering as a Disciple
1. Scientific Foundation- Laws of nature, how things interact, energy flow, material behavior from PCB.
2. Mathematical modelling- to model systems, predict outcome and optimize performance 
3. Technical Knowledge- domain knowledge. e.g- CS -> Algorithms, Data Structures, OS, Databases, etc.
4. Design thinking- Think creatively and logically. define the problem, brainstorm the solution, model and simulate design, and improve through iteration
5. Experimentation and Analysis- validate theories, collect data, analyze results in real world scenario
6. Innovation and Research- develop new solutions, which are efficient, improve the manufacturing, and advancing technology via research

---

### Engineering as a Profession
Practical application to design, develop, operate and maintain systems, products and services that serve the needs of society
#### Characteristics
- practical Application- applying the knowledge to real world to solve problems
- professional responsibility- solving problems with ethically, with responsibility. e.g- A civil engineer ensures safety, sustainability and compliant with regulations
- societal benefit- Contributing towards the society
- career oriented focus
- professional regulation

---
### Engineering working frameworks
1. Define the problem- by asking problem
2. Background Research
3. Understand and specify the requirements
4. Ideate, brainstorm
5. Evaluate the idea
6. prototype
7. Evaluate and test the prototype
8. Communicate solution
