# Six Sigma Approach
- **Six Sigma** is a **data-driven methodology** for improving business processes.
- It aims to:
    - Eliminate **defects**
    - Reduce **process variability**
    - Improve **quality and efficiency**
    - Support **business growth**

## Key Features
- **Initiated by:** Motorola in **1987**
- Uses a **disciplined and structured approach**
- Combines:
    - **Statistical tools**
    - **Non-statistical tools**
- Applied through systematic **process improvement and design methodologies**.

## Applications
Six Sigma is widely used in:
- Manufacturing
- Service industries
- Healthcare
- Other business sectors

## Main Objective

> **Reduce variation + eliminate defects → Improve quality, efficiency, and performance**

### Key Point

**Six Sigma = Data-driven process improvement methodology focused on reducing defects and variability.**

## Key Objectives
- Reduce defects
- Improve process capability and performance
- Enhance customer satisfaction
- Lower operational costs

## Core Principles of Six Sigma
- Customer driven- Delivering what customer values the most
- Data driven decision making- using analytics and stats for decision making
- Process oriented- Improving and controlling business process
- Proactive management- Preventing issues before they occur
- Collaboration- Teamwork and cross-functional engagement
- Continuous improvement- Effort to increase performance

### DMAIC Methodology
D - Define the problem, goals and requirements
M - Measure performance by collecting data
A - Analyze RCA, defects, etc.
I - Improve performance, eliminate defects
C - Control Monitor and observe performance to control the gains

### DMADV Methodology
D- Define
M- Measure
A- Analyze
D- Design
V- Verify

### The 7 tools for Six Sigma Approach
1. Cause Effect Diagram- identify root cause of problem
2. Histogram- for frequency distribution
3. Check sheet- collect and organize data
4. Control Chart- monitor process variation
5. Pareto Chart- identify and prioritize (80/20 principle)
6. Scatter Diagram- correlation b/w 2 entities
7. Flowchart- visualize steps in a process
