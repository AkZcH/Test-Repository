#### An engineer is-
A problem solver
A solution designer
A change agent
## 1. Who is a Practicing Engineer?
A **practicing engineer** is a person who applies engineering principles to solve real-world problems and actively works in the engineering profession.

A practicing engineer can be:
- A **Professional Engineer (PE):** A licensed engineer authorised to provide engineering services to the public.
- An engineer gaining professional experience under the supervision of a PE.
## 2. Attributes of a Practicing Engineer
A practicing engineer should possess the following qualities:
#### Core Attributes
1. **Technical Proficiency:** Strong knowledge of engineering principles and concepts.
2. **Ethical Integrity:** Follows professional ethics and codes of conduct.
3. **Problem-Solving Skills:** Uses analytical and innovative thinking to solve problems.
4. **Communication Skills:** Communicates effectively through speaking and writing.
5. **Teamwork and Collaboration:** Works effectively with people from different disciplines.
6. **Adaptability and Lifelong Learning:** Continuously learns and keeps up with technological advancements.
#### Additional Attributes
7. **Project Management:** Plans, executes, and manages projects.
8. **Decision-Making Under Constraints:** Balances cost, time, quality, and safety.
9. **Social and Environmental Responsibility:** Considers sustainability and the impact of engineering solutions on society.
10. **Leadership Qualities:** Guides, influences, and mentors others.
---
## 3. Core Functions of a Practicing Engineer
1. **Design and Development:** Creates products, processes, and systems.
2. **Analysis and Testing:** Evaluates performance and ensures quality.
3. **Implementation and Operation:** Supervises production and day-to-day operations.
4. **Maintenance and Support:** Ensures long-term functionality and reliability.
---
## 4. Engineer as a Problem Solver
Engineers identify real-world problems and develop practical solutions using a systematic approach.

### Engineering Method
The engineering method consists of six phases:

|Phase|Description|
|---|---|
|1. Idea|Identify the problem.|
|2. Concept|Study existing solutions, requirements, and constraints.|
|3. Planning|Define objectives and prepare a plan and schedule.|
|4. Design|Create drawings, models, schematics, and algorithms.|
|5. Development|Build prototypes, conduct experiments, and verify results.|
|6. Launch|Implement the solution and evaluate the results.|

**Key idea:** Engineering problem-solving involves identifying a problem, planning a solution, developing it, testing it, and implementing it.

---
## 5. Engineer as a Designer
An engineer acts as a designer by following a systematic process to develop solutions to problems.

#### The **engineering design process** involves:
1. **Identify the Problem:** Understand what needs to be solved.
2. **Brainstorm Solutions:** Generate possible solutions.
3. **Select a Design:** Choose the most suitable solution.
4. **Build a Prototype:** Create a working model or sample.
5. **Test and Evaluate:** Check whether the design works as expected.
6. **Optimize the Design:** Identify weaknesses and improve the design.
7. **Share the Solution:** Present or implement the final solution.

**Important:** The design process is iterative. If testing reveals problems, the engineer goes back and improves the design.

---
## 6. Engineer as a Change Agent
A **change agent** is someone who identifies opportunities for improvement, introduces innovation, implements new solutions, and influences others towards positive transformation.

### Key Aspects of an Engineer as a Change Agent
**1. Technological Transformation**
- Engineers introduce new technologies to improve existing systems.
- Example: Introducing solar and wind energy as alternatives to fossil fuels.

**2. Solving Societal Challenges**
- Engineers develop solutions to problems such as clean water, climate change, and healthcare access.
- Example: Engineers Without Borders (EWB) develops low-cost water purification systems for rural areas.

**3. Sustainable Development**
- Engineers promote environmentally friendly designs and sustainable practices.
- Examples: Green buildings, biodegradable materials, and energy-efficient transport.

**4. Economic Growth**
- Engineers improve productivity and support economic development through efficient products and systems.
- Example: Automation in manufacturing improves production and global supply chains.

**5. Digital Innovation**
- Engineers develop digital technologies, smart systems, and AI-based solutions.
- Example: Software engineers developing health applications that monitor and predict diseases.

**6. Disaster Management and Recovery**
- Engineers design systems for early warnings, rescue operations, and reconstruction.
- Example: Structural engineers designing earthquake-resistant buildings.
