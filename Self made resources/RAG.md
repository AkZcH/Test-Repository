# The Existing Problems with LLMs


Store factual knowledge in their parameters and achieve State of The Art results after fine tuning for specific NLP tasks.
But they don't perform well when access to or manipulation of knowledge is required. So their performance on knowledge-intensive tasks  is limited and lags behind task-specific architectures

They hallucinate
Struggle to access and apply knowledge
Difficult to update

# The Problem Statement
How do you make an LLM achieve the same performance as that of a task specific architecture model without modifying the LLM architecture?
