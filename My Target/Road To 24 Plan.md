# 11. Mistakes that could destroy this ambition

I'm going to be particularly direct here.

1. Chasing followers instead of expertise.

You can manufacture an impressive-looking profile. You cannot manufacture engineering competence.

2. Learning too many technologies simultaneously.

You have interests in Rust, Go, Java, AI, cloud, cybersecurity, distributed systems and more.

That's a lot.

You need depth, not an endless collection of introductory knowledge.

3. Building projects without finishing them.

A completed, benchmarked, documented distributed system is worth more than ten unfinished repositories.

4. Becoming a motivational tech influencer.

Don't spend your time telling people how ambitious you are. Show them what you have built.

5. Faking professional status.

Don't imply you work at AWS before you actually do. Don't exaggerate project metrics or conference appearances. Reputation takes years to build and seconds to destroy.

6. Neglecting communication.

An excellent engineer who cannot explain complex systems clearly has limited influence.

Learn to write, speak, present and explain.
