## 9. The audience-building part nobody wants to hear

Posting alone is not enough.

You need to become part of the **conversation around your niche**.

Every day, spend ~30 minutes:

Read posts from strong AI infra / distributed systems / cloud engineers.

Leave **3 to 5 genuinely technical comments**.

Not:

> Great post! 🔥

Instead:

> "The interesting part here is that batching improves throughput but can actually hurt tail latency. I'd expect the tradeoff to become particularly visible around P95/P99 under bursty workloads."

Now people can discover you through other people's audiences.

That's how you build a network rather than just dumping posts into the void.
